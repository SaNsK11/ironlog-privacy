IronLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

IronLog is a local-first workout tracker. Your data lives on your device. We do not run servers that store your personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Workout sessions, exercises, and set logs
  • Body measurements and progress photos
  • Your profile (name, age, fitness goals, unit preference)
  • AI-generated reports you choose to save

All of this data stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded to our servers.

AI Features

When you use Physique Scan, Weekly Report, or the AI Workout Plan, the relevant data (photos or workout stats) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate insights.

  • Anthropic processes the data per their privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy
  • We do not retain a copy on any IronLog server
  • You will be asked for permission before any data is sent to AI
  • AI-generated results are for informational purposes only and are not medical advice

Crash Reporting

IronLog uses Apple MetricKit to collect anonymous crash diagnostics. This data cannot be used to identify you personally and stays within Apple's ecosystem.

Camera & Photo Library

Camera access is used to take progress photos for physique scanning and to add a background photo to your shareable workout card. Photos are not uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly use the Physique Scan feature, in which case the photo is sent to the Claude API for analysis only.

No Account Required

IronLog does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no email login and no profile linked to an external identity.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Profile → Data → Delete All Data. This removes all workout history, measurements, and profile information from your device immediately.

Children

IronLog is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
