IronLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

IronLog is a local-first workout tracker. Your data is stored on your device and optionally synced to secure cloud servers so you can restore it on a new device. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Account & Sign In

IronLog supports two sign-in methods: Sign in with Apple, and email sign-in (a one-time code sent to your email address). Your credentials are used for authentication only. Your account is managed via Supabase, a secure cloud platform.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Workout sessions, exercises, and set logs
  • Body measurements and progress photos
  • Your profile (date of birth, biological sex, fitness goals, unit preferences)
  • AI-generated reports you choose to save

Cloud Sync (Supabase)

When you are signed in, the following data is automatically synced to our Supabase cloud database so you can restore it on a new device:

  • Workout sessions, exercise logs, and set logs
  • Body measurements
  • Your profile (fitness goals, body stats, date of birth, unit preferences)
  • Your email address (used for authentication)
  • Your chosen username (visible to squad members in leaderboards)

Your data is protected by Row-Level Security — only your account can read or write your records. See supabase.com/privacy.

Squad & Leaderboard (CloudKit)

If you join or create a Squad, the following data is written to Apple's CloudKit public database and is visible to other members of your squad:

  • Your chosen display name
  • Weekly workout volume snapshots (total sets and volume per muscle group)

No other personal information is shared. You can leave a squad at any time from the Squad tab.

AI Features

When you use Physique Scan, Weekly Report, or the AI Workout Plan, the relevant data (photos or workout stats) is sent through our Cloudflare Worker proxy to Anthropic's Claude API to generate insights.

  • Our Worker verifies your identity via your Supabase session — your data is never processed anonymously.
  • Anthropic processes the data per their privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • We do not retain a copy of AI inputs or outputs on any IronLog server.
  • AI-generated results are for informational purposes only and are not medical advice.

Camera & Photo Library

Camera access is used to take progress photos for physique scanning and to add a photo to your shareable workout card. Photos are not uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly use the Physique Scan feature, in which case the photo is sent to the Claude API for analysis only.

Notifications

IronLog may send you local notifications for rest timers during workouts and occasional comeback reminders if you haven't logged a workout in a while. No notification data is shared with third parties. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Crash Reporting

IronLog uses Apple MetricKit to collect anonymous crash diagnostics. This data cannot be used to identify you personally and stays within Apple's ecosystem.

Deleting Your Data

You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Profile → Data → Delete All Data. This removes all workout history, measurements, and profile information from your device and from our Supabase cloud database. Squad display names and weekly snapshots stored in CloudKit can be removed by leaving your squad before deleting.

Children

IronLog is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
